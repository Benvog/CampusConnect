1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

CampusConnect is a safety-first, campus-verified dating and social discovery
platform built exclusively for university students in Kenya. Unlike
general-purpose dating apps, CampusConnect restricts access to enrolled
students verified through institutional email, creating a trusted,
closed-network environment designed for meaningful connections within and
across campuses.

The platform addresses a clear gap: campus social scenes are rich but
fragmented. Students struggle to meet peers outside their immediate circles,
existing apps carry safety concerns and lack campus context, and social events
remain poorly discoverable. CampusConnect solves all three with verified
profiles, contextual matching, and a campus events layer.

2. PROBLEM STATEMENT

2.1 The Challenge
------------------
University students are at a prime life stage for forming meaningful
relationships, yet the tools available to them are either too broad (mainstream
dating apps with unverified strangers) or too passive (social media with no
matching intent). Key pain points include:

  • Safety anxiety: meeting strangers from unverified apps, especially for
    female students
  • Social silos: friend groups rarely overlap across faculties, years, or
    hostels
  • Poor event discoverability: campus events are scattered across WhatsApp
    groups and notice boards
  • Stigma: dating apps feel impersonal and carry social stigma in close-knit
    campus cultures

2.2 Opportunity
----------------
A campus-exclusive platform removes the stranger-danger element entirely.
Every user is a verified student — this single constraint transforms the trust
calculus and opens the door to richer, campus-contextual features that general
apps cannot offer.

5.2 Core Feature Specifications
---------------------------------

F1 — Student Verification
  Every account must be verified with an active .ac.ke or university
  institutional email. An OTP is sent to the address; unverified accounts
  cannot access the discovery feed. No exceptions. Profile photos are
  AI-screened for nudity at upload time.

F2 — Profile
  Required: display name, profile photo (1–6 images), university, faculty,
  year of study.
  Optional: bio (max 150 chars), pronouns, relationship intent
  (friendship / casual / serious / open), interests (multi-select).
  Location: city-level only — no precise GPS collection.

F3 — Discovery & Matching
  • Card-based swipe interface (right = like, left = pass, up = super like)
  • Match occurs on mutual right-swipe — both users notified
  • Default filter: same campus, within ±2 years of study
  • Free users: 30 right-swipes/day; premium: unlimited

F4 — Chat
  • Text messaging between matched users only
  • Photo sharing (screenshotting triggers notification to both parties)
  • Message requests expire after 7 days if ignored
  • Unmatch and report available at all times within the chat

F5 — Safety & Moderation
  • In-app report with category selection (harassment, fake profile,
    explicit content, other)
  • Reported users enter a review queue; 3 confirmed reports in 30 days
    = automatic temporary suspension
  • Block is immediate and mutual
  • Human moderation team reviews severe reports within 24 hours

6. KEY USER FLOWS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

6.1 Onboarding Flow
--------------------
  1. Download app / visit web → Enter institutional email
  2. OTP verification → Set password
  3. Complete profile (photos, bio, interests, intent)
  4. Set discovery preferences (campus, year range, intent)
  5. Begin discovery feed → First swipe experience

6.2 Match to Conversation Flow
--------------------------------
  User swipes right → mutual like detected → match animation displayed →
  system sends icebreaker prompt to both users → either user sends first
  message → conversation thread opens.

  Low user density per campus        High       Launch at 1–2 campuses only;
  (cold start)                                  partner with student associations

                                                reporting, 24hr moderation,
                                                panic link to campus security

  Institutional email resistance     Medium     Pre-negotiate with university ICT
                                                departments; offer admin dashboard

  Data breach / privacy violation    High       Encryption at rest and in transit,
                                                minimal data collection, KNDPA
                                                compliance, regular audits

  User churn after initial curiosity Medium     Events board, match nudges, weekly
                                                digest emails for re-engagement

  Revenue below projection           Medium     Reassess pricing at Month 4 based
                                                on actual conversion data

12. ASSUMPTIONS & CONSTRAINTS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

12.1 Assumptions
-----------------
  • Target universities have institutional email infrastructure (.ac.ke)
  • Students have smartphones and reasonable mobile data access
  • M-PESA penetration among students is sufficient for payment processing
  • Sufficient student demand validated through pre-launch surveys

12.2 Constraints
-----------------
  • Must comply with Kenya's Data Protection Act 2019 and university policies
  • Verification relies solely on institutional email (no student ID DB access)
  • MVP must be achievable within a 3-month window with a small team (3–5 eng.)
  • Phase 1 growth must rely heavily on organic/viral mechanisms
